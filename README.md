
# Exploration of AI Use Case: Case Studies on Self-Driving Cars
# Date:30/10/26
## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

## THEORY

A self-driving car is an AI-enabled vehicle that uses sensors, cameras, and intelligent algorithms to detect its surroundings and perform driving tasks with reduced or no human intervention, depending on its automation level.

Self-driving cars combine several AI technologies to identify roads, detect pedestrians and other vehicles, recognize traffic signals, avoid obstacles, plan routes, and make driving decisions. These technologies help improve transportation efficiency and driving safety.

## WORKING PRINCIPLE

The basic working process of an AI-enabled self-driving car is:

**Sensors and Cameras → Data Collection → Object Detection → Environment Perception → Decision Making → Path Planning → Vehicle Control → Safe Navigation**

1. Sensors and cameras collect information about the vehicle's surroundings.
2. The collected data is processed by the onboard computing system.
3. Computer vision identifies roads, vehicles, pedestrians, and traffic signs.
4. AI algorithms analyze the surroundings and understand the driving situation.
5. Machine Learning models help classify objects and predict the movement of other road users.
6. The decision-making system determines suitable driving actions.
7. Path-planning algorithms select a safe route and driving trajectory.
8. Vehicle control systems operate the steering, acceleration, and braking.
9. The vehicle continuously monitors its surroundings and adjusts its actions while driving.

## CHARACTERISTICS OF AI-ENABLED SELF-DRIVING CARS

1. **Environment Perception:** Detects roads, vehicles, pedestrians, and obstacles.
2. **Object Recognition:** Identifies traffic signs, signals, and other road users.
3. **Decision Making:** Selects appropriate driving actions based on road conditions.
4. **Path Planning:** Determines a suitable path to reach the destination.
5. **Obstacle Avoidance:** Detects obstacles and plans actions to avoid collisions.
6. **Adaptive Speed Control:** Adjusts speed according to traffic and road conditions.
7. **Lane Detection:** Identifies lane markings and helps maintain the vehicle's position.
8. **Navigation:** Uses maps, positioning systems, and route-planning algorithms to reach destinations.
9. **Predictive Analysis:** Estimates the possible movements of nearby vehicles and pedestrians.
10. **Real-Time Processing:** Continuously processes sensor data and updates driving decisions.

## RELATED AI TOOLS / TECHNOLOGIES

- **Machine Learning (ML):** Learns patterns from driving data to support object recognition and prediction.
- **Deep Learning (DL):** Processes complex visual and sensor data for driving-related tasks.
- **Computer Vision:** Identifies roads, vehicles, pedestrians, lane markings, and traffic signs.
- **Sensor Fusion:** Combines information from cameras, radar, LiDAR, and other sensors to improve environmental understanding.
- **LiDAR Technology:** Uses laser pulses to measure distances and create a three-dimensional representation of the surroundings.
- **Radar Technology:** Detects nearby objects and measures their distance and relative speed.
- **Path Planning:** Determines a suitable and safe trajectory for the vehicle.
- **Decision-Making Algorithms:** Select driving actions based on traffic conditions and detected objects.
- **Global Positioning System (GPS):** Helps determine the vehicle's location and supports navigation.
- **Vehicle Control Systems:** Convert planned actions into steering, acceleration, and braking commands.

## RESULT

AI-enabled self-driving cars demonstrate the application of Artificial Intelligence in modern transportation. By combining machine learning, computer vision, sensor fusion, path planning, and decision-making algorithms, these vehicles can perceive their surroundings, plan routes, and control driving actions. These technologies have the potential to improve road safety, transportation efficiency, and driving convenience. However, safe operation depends on the vehicle's automation capabilities, system reliability, and operating conditions.
